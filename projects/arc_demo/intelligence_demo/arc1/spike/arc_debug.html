Capacity gates — condition-atom guards (reactive to the selected task)

A capacity is available only when its guard passes. Guards are booleans over shared condition atoms (grid-Δ, palette-Δ, operand-type), composed with AND/OR — mirroring the core input_group {all_required | any_of | fold}. Atoms light up green when satisfied for the selected task; an enabled capacity turns green. Source of truth: arc_gates.py.
